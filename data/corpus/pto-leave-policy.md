This policy explains how paid time off accrues at Northfield Systems, how to request time off, guidance on company blackout periods, and the basics of parental and medical leave. It applies to all full time employees; contractors follow the terms of their individual agreements instead.

## Accrual

Full time employees accrue PTO at 1.25 days per month, equal to 15 days per year, during their first 2 years of employment. Starting in year 3, accrual increases to 1.67 days per month, equal to 20 days per year. PTO accrues monthly and is available for use as it accrues rather than in a single annual grant. Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar year; balances above that are forfeited on December 31 unless local law requires otherwise.

## Requesting Time Off

Submit PTO requests through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for single days and at least 3 weeks in advance for requests longer than 5 consecutive days. Your manager approves or declines the request in the portal; requests not addressed within 5 business days are automatically escalated to the manager's own manager. Same day sick time does not require advance notice, only a same day notification to your manager.

## Blackout Guidance

Finance Operations observes a PTO blackout during year end close, typically the first 10 business days of January, when only pre-approved exceptions are granted. Teams supporting a major product launch may declare a temporary blackout with at least 2 weeks notice to affected employees; blackout periods never apply retroactively to already approved PTO.

## Parental Leave

Northfield Systems provides 12 weeks of paid parental leave for the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child, available to both primary and secondary caregivers. Parental leave can be taken continuously or split into two blocks within the first 12 months, with at least 4 weeks notice to People Ops before each block begins where the timing is foreseeable.

## Medical Leave

Medical leave of up to 26 weeks is available for an employee's own serious health condition, coordinated through People Ops and, where applicable, in compliance with local and federal leave law. Medical leave requires documentation from a healthcare provider submitted directly to People Ops, never to your manager, to keep medical details separate from your employment file. Contact People Ops as early as possible when a medical leave is anticipated so coverage and return to work planning can begin.
