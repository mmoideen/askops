This policy defines spending limits by category, receipt requirements, the approval chain, and reimbursement timelines for Northfield Systems employees. It applies to both corporate card purchases and out of pocket expenses submitted for reimbursement. Expenses that do not comply with this policy may be rejected or clawed back through payroll.

## Spending Limits by Category

| Category | Domestic Limit | International Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Meals | $75 per day | $100 per day |
| Ground transportation | $60 per day | $80 per day |
| Lodging | $250 per night | $350 per night |
| Client entertainment | $150 per person | $200 per person |

Limits are per person per day unless otherwise noted. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client entertainment expense and is capped at $50 of the total per person limit.

## Receipts and Documentation

Any single expense over $25 requires an itemized receipt uploaded to ExpenseFlow within 14 calendar days of the purchase. Credit card statements alone are not accepted as documentation. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the purchase date require director approval regardless of amount and may be denied at Finance Operations discretion.

## Approval Chain

Expenses under $500 require manager approval only. Expenses between $500 and $5,000 require director approval in addition to manager approval. Anything over $5,000, including bulk equipment purchases or offsite event costs, requires VP approval and should be discussed with Finance Operations before the expense is incurred, not after. Travel related expenses follow the additional approval steps in the Travel Booking Policy.

## Corporate Card Rules

Employees become eligible for a corporate card after 90 days of tenure; requests go through the NSD-FIN queue. Corporate cards are for business use only. Personal charges must be reimbursed to Northfield Systems within 5 business days of the statement close or the card is suspended pending repayment. A second suspension in a rolling 12 month period results in permanent card revocation.

## Reimbursement Timelines

Properly documented expense reports submitted through ExpenseFlow are processed within 10 business days of final approval. Reports missing receipts or coded to the wrong cost center are returned to the submitter, and the clock restarts once resubmitted. Direct deposit reimbursements typically post within 2 business days of processing; paper check requests, available only by exception, take an additional 5 to 7 business days.
