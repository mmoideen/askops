This policy explains who is eligible to work remotely at Northfield Systems, the equipment stipend available for a home workspace, minimum workspace and network requirements, and the rules around updating your work location for tax purposes. Remote work arrangements are reviewed annually and are not automatically permanent.

## Eligibility

Remote work eligibility is role-based, determined by whether a role has been designated remote-eligible in the job architecture maintained by People Ops. An employee requests a remote arrangement through the HR portal, and both the direct manager and People Ops must approve before the arrangement begins. Roles requiring regular access to secured lab or data center floors are generally not eligible for full remote status, though hybrid arrangements are considered case by case.

## Equipment Stipend

Approved remote employees receive a one time $500 equipment stipend for home office setup, covering items such as a monitor, chair, or desk, submitted through the Expense Policy reimbursement process rather than a separate system. In addition, remote employees receive an ongoing $40 per month connectivity stipend added to payroll to offset home internet costs. The equipment stipend can be requested again after 3 years, but not sooner, even if the employee moves or changes roles.

## Workspace Requirements

Remote employees must have a dedicated workspace with a door or partition sufficient for private video calls, since client and HR conversations often happen over the same video bridge tools used internally. Home networks must use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption; open or WEP secured networks do not meet the minimum standard. Production access, whether standing or JIT as described in Production Access Request, must never be used over public Wi-Fi, including hotel and coffee shop networks, even when connected through GlobalConnect; use a personal hotspot instead in those situations.

## Tax Location Rules

Your work location on file in the HR system determines payroll tax withholding, so any change to where you primarily work, including a permanent move, must be updated in the HR system within 30 days of the change. Working from a different state or country temporarily, such as an extended stay with family, for more than 20 consecutive business days in a rolling 12 month period must also be reported to People Ops in advance, since it can trigger tax withholding obligations even without a permanent move. Failure to report a location change within the required window can result in incorrect tax withholding that becomes the employee's responsibility to resolve.

## Equipment and Laptop Considerations

Remote employees follow the standard hardware tiers and shipping process described in Laptop Provisioning; there is no separate remote-specific hardware track, though shipping to a residential address rather than an office is the default for remote employees.
