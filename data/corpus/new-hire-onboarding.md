This checklist walks new Northfield Systems employees and their managers through the first week: account provisioning, badge issuance, the buddy program, and required trainings. Managers should review this document with their new hire on day one rather than assuming it will be read independently.

## Before Day One

People Ops provisions a NorthID account 3 business days before the start date, which automatically creates a northfield.example email address and enrolls the new hire in baseline Relay channels. Laptop shipment is triggered at the same time; see Laptop Provisioning for hardware tiers and shipping timelines. Hiring managers must submit the equipment tier request no later than 5 business days before the start date to avoid a delayed delivery.

## Day One

Arrive by 9:00 AM for badge photo and building access setup at the front desk. Badge access defaults to standard floors only; lab and data center floors require a separate request through the NSD-ITOPS queue with manager approval. After badge issuance, new hires complete laptop activation, which includes GlobalConnect VPN installation and NorthAuth MFA enrollment; follow the VPN Setup Guide during this step. IT Operations staffs a walk-in desk on the 3rd floor from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM every Monday specifically for new hire setup.

## First Week Checklist

1. Complete NorthID password setup and MFA enrollment.
2. Install GlobalConnect and confirm VPN connectivity to vpn1.northfield.internal.
3. Join required Relay channels: #general, #it-help, and your team channel. See the Team Chat Usage Guidelines for naming conventions before creating any new channel.
4. Review the Expense Policy and PTO Leave Policy with your manager.
5. Meet your assigned onboarding buddy (see below).
6. Complete Security Awareness 101 by day 5.

## Buddy Program

Every new hire is paired with a buddy from their team, assigned by the hiring manager before day one. The buddy is not the manager and is expected to check in daily for the first two weeks, then weekly through the end of the first 30 days. Buddies help with informal questions, such as meeting norms and unwritten team practices, that do not belong in formal documentation.

## Required Trainings

Security Awareness 101 is due by day 5 and covers phishing recognition; see the Phishing Reporting Guide for how to report suspicious emails after training. Code of Conduct training is due by day 10. Data Handling and Classification training is due by day 14 and is a prerequisite for requesting production access later in the role; see Production Access Request for what comes after this training. Managers receive an automated reminder in Relay if any training is incomplete by its due date, and incomplete mandatory training after 30 days is escalated to the department director.
