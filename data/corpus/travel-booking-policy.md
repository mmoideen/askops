This policy explains how to book business travel for Northfield Systems, including the required booking tool, class of service rules by flight length, per diem rates, and the approval steps for international travel. All business travel must be booked through TravelDesk to remain eligible for reimbursement and corporate insurance coverage; travel booked outside TravelDesk without a pre-approved exception is not reimbursable.

## Booking Tool

TravelDesk is available at travel.northfield.internal and is the only approved channel for booking flights, hotels, and rental cars on Northfield's behalf. Bookings made through TravelDesk are automatically coded to the traveler's cost center and do not require a separate expense report for the base fare and lodging; only incidentals such as meals go through the Expense Policy reimbursement process.

## Class of Service Rules

| Flight Length | Domestic | International |
|---|---|---|
| Under 6 hours | Economy | Economy |
| 6 to 9 hours | Economy | Premium economy |
| Over 9 hours | Not applicable | Business |

Upgrades beyond the approved class of service are the traveler's own expense and cannot be reimbursed or paid for with a corporate card. Exceptions for medical accommodation are handled directly by People Ops rather than through TravelDesk.

## Per Diem

Per diem for incidentals not covered by a direct booking is $60 per day for domestic travel and $90 per day for international travel, prorated for partial travel days at 75 percent of the daily rate. Per diem is intended to cover minor incidentals; it does not replace the meal limits defined in the Expense Policy, and travelers should not claim both a full per diem and itemized meals for the same day.

## International Travel Approval

International trips require VP approval submitted through TravelDesk at least 2 weeks before departure. Trips to countries on the current restricted travel list, maintained by Security Engineering and linked from the TravelDesk homepage, require additional sign off from Security Engineering regardless of trip purpose. Approval requests submitted with less than 2 weeks notice are not guaranteed to be processed before the intended travel date, and Finance Operations is not obligated to expedite them.

## Cancellations and Changes

Cancel or change a booking directly in TravelDesk rather than contacting the airline or hotel directly, since direct changes may not sync back to Northfield's records and can cause a booking to appear unused for insurance purposes. Same day changes made outside TravelDesk during active travel are the one exception and should be logged in TravelDesk within 24 hours after the fact.
